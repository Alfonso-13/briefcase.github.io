The source code in this repository is distributed under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

The license does not grant rights to impersonate the portfolio owner or reuse personal identity material. Local portfolio configuration, personal biographies, résumés, photographs, custom portraits, logos and professional records must be replaced in forks unless their owner grants separate permission.

The repository intentionally tracks only fictitious starter content under `src/assets/config.example/`. A local `src/assets/config/` directory is generated from that template when no private configuration exists and remains ignored by Git.

Third-party names, logos, trademarks and profile-service artwork remain subject to the rights of their respective owners. Their presence in a demonstration does not transfer ownership or provide a separate redistribution license.
